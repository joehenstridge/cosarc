About Us

City of Salisbury Athletics & Running Club (CoSARC) is a volunteer-run, not-for-profit club welcoming athletes and runners of all abilities, from Year 4 (age 8+) through to seniors. We compete across Hampshire, Wiltshire and the South West, train from our own floodlit track near Five Rivers Leisure Centre, and organise two flagship community events each year: the Salisbury 10 Mile and the Stonehenge Stomp.

Registered office: c/o CoSARC, Kitley, The Street, West Winterslow, Salisbury, SP5 1SX
