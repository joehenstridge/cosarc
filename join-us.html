Join CoSARC

We're a friendly, volunteer-run, not-for-profit club, and we welcome athletes and runners of all abilities — your first two sessions are completely free, so come and try us out.

Membership categories

Quad Kids · Years 5–6

Full for 2026/7

Coached sprinting, running, jumping and throwing across the four Quad Kids events: 75m sprint, 600m run, long jump and howler throw.

Junior Multi-Events · Years 7–8

Full for 2026/7

Sprints, long jump, shot put, high jump, hurdles, discus, pole vault and javelin.

Junior Middle Distance · Years 9–13

Training for 800m, 1500m, 1600m and 3000m track running.

Junior Track & Field · Year 9+

Sprints, hurdles, jumps, pole vault and throws.

Seniors · 18+

Middle distance, distance running (road, trail and cross country) and track & field, with coached track intervals and group runs for every ability.

How to join

Email membership@salisbury-arc.org with your name, age or school year, and which section you'd like to join.
