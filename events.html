Events

CoSARC organises two flagship events each year, and our members compete in leagues and championships across Hampshire, Wiltshire and the South West.

Road Race · Flagship Event

Salisbury 10 Mile

A friendly race suitable for all abilities, following a scenic route north of Salisbury along the banks of the River Avon through the gently undulating Woodford Valley — with fine views of the Cathedral spire on the return leg. UKA-licensed, part of the Hampshire & District Road Race League, and entirely organised and marshalled by volunteers.

Walking Event · January

Stonehenge Stomp

Our long-running annual walk with multiple distance routes around Stonehenge, using a safe A303 crossing via underpass or footbridge. Places are strictly limited out of respect for the landscape and local communities — recent years have sold out within days, so book early. Online entry only; no day-of or postal entries.

Contact: stomp@salisbury-arc.org
